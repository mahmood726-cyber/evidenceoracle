Data Sources

EvidenceOracle analyzes 403 Cochrane systematic reviews by combining features from 6 to 10 data sources:

  • MetaAudit (primary): Automated audit of 4,424 Cochrane meta-analyses providing audit flags, failure/warning counts, and percentage of failed checks.
  • Robustness analysis: Multiverse-style sensitivity analysis computing robustness scores, fragility, bias, prediction interval, and ORB sub-scores.
  • Benford analysis: Mean absolute deviation from Benford's Law distribution as a statistical integrity indicator.
  • Changepoint detection: Structural breaks in cumulative meta-analysis time series indicating temporal instability.
  • Study-level metadata: Number of studies (k), total sample size (total_n), data type.
  • Quality scoring: Composite quality grade from bias assessment, methodological rigour, and reporting completeness.

Feature Engineering

20 features were engineered from the raw data sources. Key categories:

  • Robustness: robustness_score, fragility_sub, bias_sub, prediction_sub, orb_sub
  • Audit: audit_n_fail, audit_n_warn, audit_pct_fail, 5 binary audit flags
  • Statistical: benford_mad, k, total_n
  • Topological: topdim_* (which multiverse dimension most affects conclusions)
  • Temporal: has_changepoint, n_changepoints, never_stabilized

Training Approach

Three classifiers trained with stratified 5-fold cross-validation (seed = 42):

  • Logistic Regression (L2 regularized): Baseline linear model
  • Random Forest (100 trees): Non-linear ensemble with built-in feature importance
  • Gradient Boosting (learning rate = 0.1): Sequential boosting ensemble

Two prediction targets:

  • Primary: fragile_or_unstable — whether the conclusion is fragile or temporally unstable
  • Secondary: low_quality — whether the review has a low composite quality score

All predictions are out-of-fold (OOF), preventing overfitting and providing honest performance estimates.

Limitations

  • Primary target AUC is moderate (~0.64 for Random Forest), reflecting inherent difficulty of predicting fragility from observable features.
  • Secondary target (low quality) is highly predictable (AUC ~0.97), suggesting quality markers are well-captured.
  • The 403-review dataset is limited; external validation on non-Cochrane reviews is needed.
  • Features from automated analyses may contain measurement noise (e.g., Benford MAD is sensitive to small k).
  • Temporal stability of predictions has not been assessed.
